    🚩 Red flags of fake opportunities

    • Asks for a registration, processing or “refundable” deposit fee.
    • Contacts you only on WhatsApp or Telegram, or from a personal Gmail or Yahoo address instead of an official domain.
    • Guarantees selection or a job, or pressures you with “only a few seats left”.
    • Asks for your OTP, PIN or card details.
    • Can’t be found on the organiser’s official website or verified social page.
